Section 01Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how TikTok Bulk Unfollow Friends & Following (“the Extension”) handles your information. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean the developer of the Extension.

The Extension helps you unfollow accounts on TikTok in bulk, such as non-followers, friends or everyone, and manage your Following list from your own signed-in TikTok tab. It works inside your browser. We never receive your TikTok data, and we never sell it.

Section 02Information we access

  • Your account: basic profile details of the TikTok account you are signed in to, such as your username, only so the Extension knows whose Following list to load. These details stay in your browser.
  • Your Following list: the accounts you follow and their public profile details, such as username, name, photo, follower count, video count, whether an account is verified or private, and whether it follows you back.
  • Your TikTok session: the Extension works in your open TikTok tab, where you are already signed in, and uses TikTok’s own website requests in the same way the TikTok site does. It never asks for, reads or stores your password.
  • Files you import: if you import a list, the file is read on your device only to select accounts or add them to your Keep list.
  • License key: if you buy Premium, the key you enter is used only to confirm your purchase.

Section 03How we use information

  • To show your list in the Following Manager with search, sort, filters and insights.
  • To unfollow only the accounts you choose, such as non-followers, friends, everyone or the accounts you select, while always skipping your Keep list.
  • To follow an account again when you ask.
  • To keep a history of removed accounts, so you can see and export your results.
  • To create the export files you ask for and save them to your downloads folder.
  • To remember your settings, such as theme, speed and run limit, to count your free unfollows and to confirm your license.

Section 04Where your data is kept

Your Keep list, unfollow history, settings, free unfollow count and license key are saved only in your browser, on your own device. Your Following list is read from TikTok while you use the Extension and is never uploaded to us or anyone else.

Export files are saved on your device and stay under your control. You can clear your history at any time, and removing the Extension deletes its saved data.

Section 05What we share

We do not share your data with anyone, with one exception: checking your license. When you activate Premium, and when you open the Extension after that, your license key and the product ID are sent to Gumroad, our payment provider, only to confirm that your license is valid. No TikTok data is included.

If you choose to buy Premium, the purchase takes place on Gumroad’s website, under Gumroad’s own terms.

Bug reports: “Report a bug” opens your own email app with a new message to us. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.

Section 06Our commitments

  • We never sell or rent your data.
  • We never use your data for advertising or credit decisions.
  • We never use your data for anything except managing who you follow on TikTok.
  • We never add tracking, analytics or code from outside the Extension.

Section 07Children

The Extension is not intended for children.
